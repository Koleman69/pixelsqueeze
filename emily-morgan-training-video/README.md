# The Emily Morgan Way – 45-minute service training video

- `Emily_Morgan_Way_45min_Video_Script.docx` – timed production script, production guide, cast, handout and knowledge check
- `graphics/` – 1920x1080 cards, lower thirds and overlays for the avatar video platform
- `assets/photos/` – hotel reference photos
- `build/` – `script_data.py` (script content), `gfx.py` (renders graphics), `build_docx.py` (builds the script)

Rebuild: `python3 build/gfx.py && python3 build/build_docx.py`

Fill in every yellow-highlighted policy item before the final render.
